The firm

A specialist criminal practice in Great Barr

Newton Law Practice is a criminal defence firm based at 30a Newton Road, Great Barr. We represent clients and businesses nationwide, advising and assisting during interviews under caution and representing clients in the Crown Courts of England and Wales.

We fiercely defend our clients while keeping complete confidentiality and professionalism, to limit any damage to your reputation. Our team has over 50 years of combined experience.

The firm is authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority as a recognised sole practice (SRA No. 321430), and was previously known as Nijjar Solicitors.
