Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 19 October 2026

At Vector Moon, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit vectormoon.co.uk, book a strategy call, or enquire about our web design services. We comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

Vector Moon is a UK-based web design studio (Coventry / West Midlands, serving clients UK-wide). We design and build bespoke websites and related digital services for businesses. This policy covers our website and client enquiries — not third-party marketplaces or other brands.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information needed to respond to enquiries and deliver our services. This may include:

  • Name and business name — so we know who we are speaking with.
  • Email address and phone number — to reply to your enquiry or call booking.
  • Project details — information you send about your current site, goals, or brief.
  • Technical data — such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected via standard web server logs and analytics tools we configure (for example Google Analytics, if enabled on the site).

We do not store payment card details on our website. If you pay for a project, payments are handled through the invoicing or payment provider we agree with you at the time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for:

  • Responding to enquiries, strategy calls, and project proposals.
  • Delivering and supporting website projects you commission.
  • Meeting legal obligations (for example tax and accounting records).
  • Improving our website and services using aggregated or anonymised insights where possible.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing emails unless you have asked us to stay in touch.

4. Sharing Your Information

We only share data with trusted providers needed to run the business, such as:

  • Hosting and email providers — who process site traffic or messages on our behalf.
  • Analytics providers — if analytics is enabled on the site.
  • Accountants or legal advisers — where required for compliance.

We never sell your personal information to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

5. International Data Transfers

Some tools we use (for example hosting, email, or analytics) may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Enquiries that do not become clients: typically up to 24 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Client project and billing records: retained for 6 years to meet UK tax and accounting requirements.

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. To exercise them, email indi@vectormoon.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our site may use essential cookies to make pages work, and analytics cookies to understand traffic if analytics is enabled. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including HTTPS on our website, restricted access to business accounts, and strong authentication where available.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are aimed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
